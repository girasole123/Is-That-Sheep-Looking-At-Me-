# Is that sheep looking at me? The game
- 2-4 Players
- 10-20 minutes
- Ages 8+
## Your mission
Build a mighty flock of sheep using
Sheep Head and Sheep Butt Cards.
But be careful, as your sheep can get
stolen or discarded by other players!

The player with the biggest flock
at the end of the game, wins!

## Game contents
- 54x Game Cards
	+ 16x two half of sheep (head & butt) of each color: white, orange, magenta, cyan, yellow, lime, pink, grey, beige, mint, purple, blue, brown, green, red
	+ 2x two half of rainbow sheep (head & butt)
- 1x Sheep Coin Token (one face is head, and the other face is butt)

## Game setup
1. Shuffle all 54 Game Cards.
2. Deal each player 5 Cards face down.
Only look at your own hand.
3. Place the remaining deck face down in
the middle of the play area, creating a
Draw Pile. Leave room for a Discard Pile.
4. Whoever saw a real life sheep most
recently goes first!

## On your turn
### Step 1 - Draw
- Always start your turn by drawing
1 Card from the Draw Pile.
- In later rounds, you may end up with
less than 3 cards in your hand. In that
case, continue drawing until you have
3 cards in your hand.

Before reading on,
see below for all Card Types.

### Step 2 - Play or Pass
- You can do many different moves,
in any order, as much or as little
as you want!
- You can even do nothing and skip
straight to Step 3.

#### Moves you can play:
- Add a Valid Sheep from your hand to
your field. (the area on the table in front of you)

	- Basic Sheep (head & butt of the same color)
	- Half rainbow (rainbow head & color butt, or color head & rainbow butt)
	- FrankenSheep (butt & butt & Franken modifier, or head & head & Franken modifier)
	- Painted Sheep (head of one color & butt of another color & )
- Play an Action Card at an opponent of
your choice.
- Play up to 1 Challenge Card against
an opponent of your choice.
- Bodyswap any sheep in any field,
including your own.
(see “BodySwapping”)

### Step 3 - Baa
- After step 2, if you have more than 7
cards in your hand, discard cards face
down until you have 7.
- Say “Baa!” to announce you have
finished your turn.
- Play moves clockwise.

### Reminder
- Step 1 - Draw: Draw 1 card,
 or draw until you have 3.
- Step 2 - Play/Pass: Play as many or as few cards as
 you’d like or skip to Step 3.
- Step 3 - Baa: Discard down to 7 cards if
needed then“Baa” to end turn.

## Card types
### Sheep cards
#### Basic sheep cards
There are 16 uniquely coloured Sheep,
split into a Head Card and Butt Card
of each colour.
Play a Head and a Butt of the same
colour together to make a Basic Sheep!
At the end of the game, each Basic Sheep
in your field is worth 1 point!

#### Rainbow Sheep Cards
There are 2 Rainbow Sheep.

split into a Head Card and Butt Card.
A Rainbow Head can be played with any
coloured Butt and a Rainbow Butt can be
played with any coloured Head!

Sheep made with 2 Rainbow Cards create
a Full Rainbow Sheep, which are worth 2
points at the end of the game.

### Modifier cards
#### Paint Cards
There are 2 Paint Cards.

Play a Paint Card with any coloured
Sheep Head and any coloured Sheep
Butt, to create a Painted Sheep, which worth 1 point.

#### Franken Cards
There are 2 Franken Cards.

Play a Franken Card with any 2 Head
Cards or any 2 Butt Cards to create a
FrankenSheep, which worth 1 point.

A Double-Butted Sheep
protects itself
and your entire flock
from Wheat Cards.

A Double-Headed Sheep
protects itself
and your entire flock
from Wolf Cards.

Keep an eye out for any Modified Sheep
on any field (including your own) as they
can be BodySwapped with!

### Action cards
Discard all Action Cards once used.

#### Yoink! Cards
There are 2 yonk cards.

Play a Yoink! Card at an
opponent of your choice
to blindly take 2 cards from
their hand and put them in your hand.

#### Wheat Cards
There are 3 wheat cards.

Play a Wheat Card to
lure any 1 Sheep from an
opponent’s field, including
any modifiers, into your field.

A double-butted FrankenSheep protects
all Sheep in its field from Wheat Cards as it
has no mouth to eat with!

#### Wolf Cards
There are 2 wolf cards.

Play a Wolf Card to remove
any 1 Sheep from an
opponent’s field, including
any modifiers, into the discard pile.

A double-headed FrankenSheep protects
all Sheep in its field from Wolf Cards as it has
eyes in the back of its heads!

#### Re-Flip Cards
There are 2 re-flip cards.

- A Coin is flipped when a
Challenge Card is played.
(see next panel)
- A Re-Flip Card forces a new guess and
the coin must be flipped again!
- You can even play a Re-Flip Card if it
doesn’t directly affect you. So, any player
can use one even if it is not its turn.

### Challange cards
These 5 chaotic cards, each with a unique
ability, require you to correctly guess
whether the Sheep Coin is looking at you
(heads) or isn’t looking at you (butts)!

- Only 1 Challenge Card can be played
per turn.
- Challenge Cards can’t be discarded until
they’ve been used.

Any Challenge Cards held
at the end of the game are each worth
MINUS 3 points. Make sure you play them!

1. Play a Challenge Card against an
opponent of your choice.
2. Declare if you think the Sheep Coin
is or isn’t looking at you.
3. Your chosen opponent flips
the Sheep Coin.
4. If you guessed correctly, use the
card’s
effect against your chosen
opponent. If you guessed incorrectly,
your chosen opponent uses the
card’s effect against you.
5. Discard the Challenge Card.

#### Lure 2 Sheep
Lure 2 Sheep of your choice from the
opponent’s field to yours (even if they
have a Double-Butted FrankenSheep).

#### Yoink! Entire Hand
Take your chosen opponent’s entire hand
and add it to yours.

#### Halve 2 Sheep
1. Steal half a Sheep of your choice from
the opponent’s field, including the
Modifier Card if it has one, and put this
in your hand.
2. Your opponent collects the remaining
half to their hand.
3. Repeat this with another sheep.

#### Remove 2 Sheep
Send 2 Sheep of your choice from the
opponent’s field to the Discard Pile
(even if they have a Double-Headed
FrankenSheep).

#### Recover 1 Sheep
Create a Sheep using cards from the
Discard Pile:
1. Set aside your own hand, temporarily.
2. Go through the Discard Pile, pick out a
valid Sheep and add it to your field. If
you want to revive a Sheep that uses
a Modifier Card, you can revive the
Modifier Card as well.
3. Return the Discard Pile and resume
your original hand.
